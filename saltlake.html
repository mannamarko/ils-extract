Overview

Established in the year 2000 under the visionary leadership of Dr. Om Tantia, ILS Hospitals Salt Lake stands tall as a beacon of healthcare excellence. Over the years, we have grown to become a renowned multispecialty hospital with a legacy of commitment to quality patient care. Here’s an overview of what sets us apart:

1. A Healing Legacy: With 85 beds and three modern operation theaters, ILS Hospitals Salt Lake is dedicated to providing compassionate and comprehensive healthcare. We take pride in our long history of serving the community with unwavering dedication.

2. Center of Excellence: Our hospital is recognized as a Center of Excellence in Bariatric and Metabolism Surgery. We offer cutting-edge treatments to address weight-related issues and metabolic disorders, ensuring a healthier future for our patients.

3. Affordable Excellence: At ILS Hospitals Salt Lake, we believe that high-quality healthcare should be accessible to all. We are committed to delivering excellent treatment at an affordable price, making world-class medical care within reach.

4. Multispecialty Expertise: Our hospital specializes in various medical disciplines, including gynecology, maternity, laparoscopic surgery, pediatrics, orthopedic care, and more. Our multidisciplinary team of experts is dedicated to providing personalized, patient-centric care across various medical domains.

5. Training for Excellence: ILS Hospitals is accredited with the prestigious Fellowship of the National Board. This recognition allows us to train post-doctoral surgeons in advanced skills, particularly in the field of minimal access surgery. Our commitment to education and training ensures that we stay at the forefront of medical advancements.

At ILS Hospitals Salt Lake, our mission is simple: to provide the highest quality healthcare services with compassion and affordability. We are dedicated to making a difference in the lives of our patients, and we take pride in our patient-first approach

Discover Comprehensive Care: Explore our website to learn more about our services, renowned medical professionals, and state-of-the-art facilities. Whether you have a specific healthcare need or are seeking wellness advice, our doors are always open to you. Feel free to reach out to us with your inquiries or to schedule an appointment. Your health is our priority.
